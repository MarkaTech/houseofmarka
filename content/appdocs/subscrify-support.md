## Common questions

### The subscribe option is not showing on my product page

Three things have to be true: the selling plan must be **published**, not a draft; the product
must be attached to that plan; and the app block has to be added once per theme —
**Online Store → Themes → Customize → Add block → Apps → Marka Subscrify**, then Save.

### A renewal charge failed

Shopify attempts the charge against the customer's payment mandate. When it fails, the app
runs your configured retry schedule and emails the customer a link to update their payment
method. If every retry fails, the contract moves to the status you chose — paused or
cancelled. The full attempt history is on the subscription's timeline.

### Can a customer change their delivery date or address themselves?

Yes, in the customer portal, within the limits you set — you decide how far ahead changes can
be made, how many skips are allowed, and whether a change needs email confirmation.

### What happens to my subscriptions if I downgrade or uninstall?

Nothing is deleted. Subscription contracts live in Shopify and survive both. Downgrading
pauses configuration beyond the new plan's limits and keeps the data. **Uninstalling stops
the app from processing renewals but does not cancel your customers' subscriptions** — decide
what should happen to active subscribers first, and tell them.

### How do I cancel?

Switch to the Free plan inside the app, or uninstall. Cancelling is handled through Shopify
billing and pro-rated by Shopify.
