## Common questions

### The widget is not showing on my product page

The app block has to be added once per theme: **Online Store → Themes → Customize → Add block → Apps → Marka Bundles**, then Save. The offer also has to be *published*, not a draft, and it must target that product or a collection it belongs to.

### The discount is not applying

Discounts attach to cart lines the widget adds. If the shopper adds items through your theme's own add-to-cart button instead of the bundle button, the bundle is not applied. Check the offer is published and its schedule window is open.

### What happens to my offers if I downgrade?

Nothing is ever deleted. Offers beyond the new plan's limits are **paused** and A/B tests are stopped with results kept. Upgrade again and press Publish and everything returns exactly as it was.

### How do I cancel?

Switch to the Free plan inside the app, or uninstall. Cancelling is handled through Shopify billing and pro-rated by Shopify.
