## Common questions

### My logo or address is not showing on documents

Both come from **Settings → Store identity** inside the app, not from your Shopify theme. Fill
in the brand name, legal entity, address, tax ID and logo URL there once, and every document
type picks them up.

### Can I change a document after it has been issued?

No, and that is deliberate. Each document is stored as a point-in-time snapshot so an issued
invoice can always be reproduced exactly as it was issued — which is the whole reason accounting
accepts it. If the underlying order changes, generate a new document, or issue a credit note
against the original.

### Someone has my document link. Can they see my other orders?

No. Each shared link carries a long random token scoped to that one document. It grants no
access to your store, your other documents or your customer list. Treat the link like any other
shareable file link and only send it to the customer it belongs to.

### How is billing counted?

Per document generated, never per untouched order. A store with 5,000 orders that prints 200
invoices is charged for 200. Charges appear on your existing Shopify invoice.

### How do I cancel?

Switch to the Free plan inside the app, or uninstall. Cancelling is handled through Shopify
billing and pro-rated by Shopify. Documents you have already issued remain viewable.
