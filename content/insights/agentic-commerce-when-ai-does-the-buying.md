The most consequential commerce shift in progress is not a new marketplace — it is a new *shopper*. AI assistants increasingly research products, compare options and complete purchases on a human's behalf. Every merchant assumption about shelves, search and persuasion is calibrated to human eyes. Agents read differently.

## What agents actually reward

An agent shopping for "a quiet dishwasher under €600 that fits a 45cm slot" does not scroll your category page or feel your brand video. It parses structured data: specifications, availability, price including delivery, returns terms, review distributions. Merchants win agent traffic on three things:

- **Machine-readable truth.** Complete structured attributes (schema.org product markup, clean feeds, accurate GTINs), honest availability, and delivery promises expressed as data. Vague listings that convert humans through photography are *invisible* to spec-driven queries.
- **Verifiable trust.** Ratings, warranty terms, seller performance — agents weight what they can verify and discount what they cannot. Fake-review-adjacent tactics stop working entirely when the buyer runs statistical checks by default.
- **Frictionless checkout.** Agent-mediated checkout flows — via emerging standards like agent-payments protocols and delegated-purchase APIs — favour merchants whose checkout is API-clean. Every dark pattern, forced account and surprise fee is a defection trigger for a buyer with zero switching cost and perfect memory.

## The near-term merchant checklist

1. **Audit your product data as if no human sees it.** Missing dimensions, inconsistent units, attributes in image text only — all fatal. This is the same [feed discipline](/insights/product-feeds-are-your-real-storefront/) marketplaces already pay for, now with higher stakes.
2. **Expose live availability and delivery.** An agent asked "arrives by Friday?" needs an answer in data. Estimated ranges scraped from a PDP footer do not survive.
3. **Keep pricing coherent across surfaces.** Agents compare everywhere simultaneously; channel price conflicts that humans never noticed become automatic lost sales — or parity-rule violations.
4. **Watch your traffic mix.** Agent-mediated sessions are already visible in some categories' logs — headless user strings, spec-heavy query patterns, zero dwell time before conversion. Measure them before deciding what they are worth to you.

## The strategic question

Agents compress discovery, which compresses differentiation-by-advertising. If the buyer's agent optimises for verified quality per euro, brand equity migrates toward *product truth* — the reviews you earned, the spec sheet that does not lie, the delivery promise you keep. Retail media as currently constructed also gets renegotiated: agents do not see sponsored placements the way people do, and the platforms know it.
